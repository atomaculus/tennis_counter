# PLAYCE Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

PLAYCE is an Android app composed of:
- a Wear OS app used to track live tennis or padel scores
- a phone companion app used for history, match details, live score display, and sharing

This policy describes how PLAYCE handles data in the current codebase.

## Data processed by the app

PLAYCE can process the following categories of data generated by your use of the app:
- match score data, including points, games, sets, and final result
- match duration
- timestamps used to save local history and coordinate sync retries
- technical sync identifiers such as `idempotencyKey`
- purchase state needed to unlock Premium features through Google Play Billing

## How the data is used

The app uses this data to:
- run the live score counter on Wear OS and mobile
- sync match results between your watch and your paired Android phone
- store match history locally on your phone when Premium access is available
- prevent duplicate inserts during watch-to-phone sync
- generate a shareable match summary image when you explicitly choose to share one
- restore and validate Premium entitlement through Google Play Billing

## Data sharing

PLAYCE does not operate its own backend for match storage.

Data is shared only in these cases:
- between your watch and paired Android phone through Google Play Services Wearable Data Layer
- with Google Play Billing to process Premium purchases
- with another app only if you explicitly use Android's share sheet to share a result image

PLAYCE does not sell your data.

## Diagnostics and analytics

The current project includes Firebase services in both app modules.

- Firebase Crashlytics may collect diagnostic information such as crash traces, app version, device model, OS version, and related technical metadata to improve app stability.
- Firebase Analytics may process general usage information related to how the app is used and how product flows perform.

Any public release must keep this policy aligned with the actual build configuration shipped to Google Play.

## Local storage

PLAYCE stores data locally on your devices:
- phone: local Room database for match history and related metadata
- watch: local pending sync state used for retry and ACK handling

You can remove locally stored data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

## Data not requested directly by PLAYCE

In the current implementation, PLAYCE does not directly require:
- account creation
- precise location
- contacts
- microphone
- camera
