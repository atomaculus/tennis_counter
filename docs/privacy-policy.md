# PLAYCE Privacy Policy (draft)

PLAYCE stores match results locally on your watch and phone.

## Data used
- Match score (points/games/sets).
- Match duration.
- Timestamps needed for local history and sync retries.

## Data sharing
- No match data is uploaded to PLAYCE servers.
- Data is exchanged only between your watch and your paired phone (Google Play Services Wear Data Layer).
- If you choose to share a result image, it is shared only through the app/provider you select.

## Storage
- Phone: local Room database for match history.
- Watch: local pending queue for reliable sync and idempotent ACK.
